## Read 1: how the native picks the squad's spawn group

### What was read

| What | Address | Notes |

| Script | `XGBattle.GetSpawnGroup`, `XGBattle_SP.GetSpawnPoints`, `XComTacticalGame` (option `SpawnGroup`), `XGBattle_SP.InitMeldContainers`, `XGBattle` (the tactical intro) | |

### What it shows

1. **The candidates.** For every `PrefabInstance` in the loaded levels' actor lists whose template's name does not
   contain `Doorway`, every live actor in its archetype-to-instance map that is an `XComSpawnPointNativeBase`
   and whose `GetUnitType()` is `UNIT_TYPE_Soldier` or `UNIT_TYPE_Tank` counts. Its key is its `Tag`.
   - Multiplayer points (`MPTeamOne`, `MPTeamTwo`) and civilians drop out by unit type.
   - The alien base's doorway prefabs (48 soldier points, archetype `AlienBaseDoorwayB_Arc*`) drop out by name.
2. **The fallback.** Only when that finds nothing, every soldier or tank point in the levels' actor lists counts,
   keyed by tag. This is the path under LWR. Its mutator destroys the map's points and spawns six new ones with the
   first landing's tag (`XComLZMutator.RespawnLZObjects`); they are in no prefab instance, and the destroyed
   originals are skipped, so there is one group.
3. **GetSpawnGroupIndex** returns `play count % number of distinct keys` (0 when there are none).
4. **FindSpawnGroup** builds a multimap from key to points. Then:
   - If `SearchTag` names a key, that group is chosen.
   - Otherwise it sorts the distinct keys by their text and takes `keys[SpawnIndex % count]`.
   - It writes the chosen tag back to `SearchTag`.
5. **Without a URL option**, `GetSpawnGroup` passes no tag (the log's `tag: None`) and the index from step 3. So the
   group is **the play count's turn through the landings' tags in name order**.
6. **With `?SpawnGroup=Group<N>`**, `XComTacticalGame` keeps the tag `Group<N>` and the index N (the text after
   "Group"). FindSpawnGroup picks the tag `Group<N>` if a group has it, else `keys[N % count]`.
7. **The index is also the Meld group and the intro's group.** `XGBattle.GetSpawnGroup()` returns the index (or N)
   to the callers that match by number:
   - `InitMeldContainers` keeps the Meld points whose `m_iSpawnGroup` equals it;
   - the tactical intro is told it as `iSpawnGroup` (Kismet `GroupNumber`, read 2).

### Checked against the packages (C0, all 97 base maps)

- **The maps' tags fit the rule.** The 22 three-landing maps tag their landings `Group0`, `Group1`, `Group2`. The
  8 two-landing maps tag them `Group0`, `Group1`.
  - Every one-landing map has a single key: its archetype's default `XComSpawnPoint` (35 maps), `Group0` (13),
    `deleteme` (13), `Prefab1` (2), `EXT_deleteme` (1) or `nodeleteme` (1).
  - So on every map, sorted index k is `Group<k>` where there are several.
- **The cooked maps keep the prefab instances.** Their `ArchetypeToInstanceMap` is saved after the tagged
  properties: a count, then archetype-to-actor pairs. Examples:
  - URB_Bar's `PrefabInstance_19` lists its ten landing actors;
  - `PrefabInstance_17` lists the Skyranger's mesh, emitter, `PointInSpace` and sound.
- **Two maps take the fallback** (Command1's character customisation point, EWI_HQAssault). CB2_MP_Blank finds
  none (F-90).

### How sure

- **High** for the rule: candidates, keys, modulo, name sort and the URL option. It is read from the code and
  agrees with every map's tags and with the log line of F-90.
- **Medium** that destroyed or taken-out actors don't count. The flag tests in `0x66E060` and the iterators are
  read, but which flags they are is inferred. LWR's landings working at all (they would otherwise land the squad
  at the destroyed originals) supports it.

### What it allows (C4, C7)

- **Moving and turning an own landing** in place changes nothing the pick depends on: the same actors, tags and
  prefab instance.
- **Adding a landing on a plain install is possible.** Clone the ten actors under a new tag that sorts after the
  others (`Group<n>`), and **also clone the landing's `PrefabInstance`**, with its archetype-to-instance map
  pointing at the clones. Actors outside a prefab instance are ignored while any prefab holds a soldier point, and
  one instance can't hold two actors of the same archetype.
  - That is new exports and names, a step for package growth.
  - The new group gets no Meld (no Meld point has its index; `InitMeldContainers` copes with none).
  - Its intro plays at the map's last group's place, or the only place (read 2), unless the intro copy is changed
    too.
  - LWR destroys a second set of the same archetypes as duplicates, so the clone is harmless there.
- **Removing a landing is cheap.** Take its `PrefabInstance` out of the level's actor list (`empty_slots`), with its
  ten actors.
  - The groups after it in name order then shift down one index, and with them their Meld points and intro
    places.
  - So either remove only the last in name order, or rename the remaining landings' tags back to
    `Group0..Group<n-1>` in order.
  - The names already exist on those maps, but Bar-like maps save no `Tag` (the archetype's `XComSpawnPoint`), so
    a rename there adds a property.
  - With every landing removed, the fallback takes any soldier point left in the level.
- **So the plan's "more or fewer landings than the map came with" can be built** for plain Enemy Within. Removal
  is small; addition is a C7-sized step of its own. Moving and turning stays the first version.
- **Land here** on a plain install: `?SpawnGroup=Group<k>` lands at that landing on every multi-landing map
  (tags `Group<k>`). On a one-landing map the option is not needed.
- **A streamed mission level holding a landing prefab would add a group.** The iterators walk every loaded level.
  C0 did not survey the streams for this.

## Read 3: the bake and a moved Skyranger; a turned evac volume

### The bake

- **Where the casters come from.** [relight.py](../../tools/bake/relight.py) `Scene` takes its casters from the
  package it is run on: `shadows.caster_triangles` places each component by its actor's saved `Location` and
  `Rotation` (`lightmap_texels.world_matrix`).
  - An edit is told as removed casters (`--remove-caster`, an edit spec's exact hides) and posed copies (an edit
    spec's `copy` with `poses`). "A moved piece is both": its triangles are added at the new pose from the map's
    own.
  - [native_copy.py](../../tools/bake/native_copy.py) runs the relight on the grown map (`dst`), with the copies,
    hides and spec it knows.
- **The Skyranger is a caster.** URB_Bar's grounded Skyranger (`XComLevelActor_138.StaticMeshComponent_151`)
  passes `shadows.is_caster`: a texture lightmap and one shadow map, shadows not turned off.
  - The drop zone's frame (`XComLevelActor_6`) is not a caster (`CastShadow` false).
- **So a full Build sees the move only if it is told it.** Nothing tells the relight about a landing moved in place
  today.
  - If `native_spawns.py` writes the new place before the relight, the scenes before and after both have the
    Skyranger at its new place. The relight finds no change: the old shadow stays in the ground's lightmap and
    shadow maps, and none falls at the new place.
  - The move must reach the relight as "remove at the old pose, add at the new", which the `Scene` already
    models. Two ways:
    - run the relight before the move is written, with the Skyranger as a removed caster and a posed copy of
      itself; or
    - give the relight an option for an actor moved in place, with its old pose for the scene before.
  - The receivers near both places, and their shadow maps, are then relit as for any moved piece.
  - The Skyranger's own lightmap keeps its old bake, as the plan says. The `--native-copy` relight (source pose to
    copy pose) would be the way to relight it later, but it wants two actors.
- **Under LWR the Skyranger is hidden and destroyed at load** (`HideDropShip`), so its baked shadow stays without
  it, as on every LWR battle today. The shadow matters on a plain install.

### A turned evac volume

- **The game editor bakes the prefab's turn into the brush.** Every landing's evac volume (`IsDropShip`) saves
  `Rotation` (0, 0, 0), and the prefab's turn is in the brush's vertices.
  - URB_Bar's and CsmallScout_Farm's Group0 brushes are 480 by 288 (half extents 240 by 144); the Farm's Group1
    and Group2, facing west and east, are 288 by 480.
  - The box is off the actor's origin by 48 units.
  - The archetype itself saves yaw -8192. The instances override it with 0.
  - This is F-86's "nothing is turned".
- **LWR turns it at every battle.** `XComLZMutator.RespawnLZObjects` spawns a new `XComBuildingVolume` with the
  map's volume as template (its brush) and `Rotation = AdjRotation(volume.Rotation)`, that is
  `StartDir × 16384 + (the volume's yaw − the frame's yaw)`.
  - The frame's yaw tells the turn baked into the brush.
  - It then empties `m_arrBuildings` and runs `InitFloorVolumes` again.
  - 335 of the 447 lines of the base maps face east, south or west.
  - So a turned evac volume is played whenever LWR takes such a line, though no package saves one.
- **What the game does with it.** The evac test is a touch of the volume (`XComUnitPawn.Touch`: `m_bInDropShip`);
  the extraction volume and `XGLevel.m_kDropship` are the volume itself. Both go by its collision, which follows
  the actor's rotation. The cursor (`XCom3DCursor`) only asks `IsDropShip`.
- **So a turned own landing is written as LWR spawns it.**
  - The volume keeps its brush. Its `Location` and the frame's take the zone's move, and its yaw becomes the
    zone's quarter turns plus its own minus the frame's.
  - **143 of the 143 evac volumes save a `Rotation`**: the write is the same size.
  - Turning the other parts needs a `Rotation` added where it is not saved: 46 of 142 frames, 48 of 146 player
    starts, 49 of 146 `PointInSpace`s, 47 of 146 first soldier points. A soldier point's turn is only its facing.
  - `Location` is saved by every frame (142; four landings have none), player start, `PointInSpace` and soldier
    point.
  - The evac volume has no floors, so no floor volumes go with it on these maps.

### How sure

- **High** for the relight's casters, the Skyranger being a caster, the baked brushes and LWR's runtime turn: all
  read from code and packages.
- **Medium** that a turned volume saved in a package behaves as LWR's spawned one does. The same actor and brush,
  only loaded rather than spawned; nothing in the script recomputes its box from the brush without the rotation.

### What it allows (C6, C7)

- **C7 writes a turned landing in place:**
  - every actor by the rigid move about the zone;
  - the evac volume's yaw as LWR's `AdjRotation`;
  - `Rotation` added where the actor lacks one;
  - the player start's `RelativeLocation` too (spawns plan 2.2).
- **The full Build passes the Skyranger's move to the relight** as remove-and-add, and orders the writes so the
  relight sees the old place first. The C6/C7 test: Bar's Skyranger moved, a receiver lightmap near its old place
  lightens and one near the new darkens.
