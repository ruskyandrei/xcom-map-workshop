An early preview: see the README for what you need and the user guide (in the release, and online at <https://ruskyandrei.github.io/xcom-map-workshop/>) for how it works.

**Download** `XCOM-Map-Workshop-0.7.0-Setup.exe` (recommended) or the zip. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.

**Upgrading from 0.6.0:** close the editor, then install over it. Your projects, prefabs, builds and the maps you installed in the game are kept. From this version on, Setup asks you to close a running editor itself.
- **Each map is read from the game again the first time you open it** (about half a minute), because the editor now reads the map's diagonal cover, background sound and triggers. **Cache everything…** (under **Settings**, the gear) with **Every map, ready to open** does them all at once.
- **A project or prefab saved in 0.7.0 can't be opened in 0.6.0.** If you pass one on, or a map pack with its project files, the other person needs 0.7.0 too.
- **A cleared map now loses its triggers too.** The next Build of a project that clears its map takes out the map's touch triggers, water volumes and sight points, and switches off the scripts they start. To keep them, open **Clear map…** again and tick **Keep the map's triggers and scripts**.
- **A map pack exported by 0.6.0 or earlier names folders of the computer that made it**, your Windows account name among them, in `packages\<map>\manifest.json` inside its zip. Export the pack again before you share it, or delete that file from the zip: nothing reads it.
- **A baked build made by 0.5.3 or earlier**, with its lighting in a `.tfc` file beside the map, has to be built again before you can play it, install it in the game or pack it.
- **Level snap is gone:** <kbd>L</kbd> is now **Align to grid**, and a floor piece is placed like any other piece, then put at a storey's height with **Level** (below).

### New features
- **Draw walls, floors, roofs and ground at any size and angle.** A drawn wall takes any length, height and thickness, with ends that can lean. Walls meet at any angle in mitred corners: hold <kbd>J</kbd> while you drag a wall's end onto another's, or pick two walls and use **Join ends**. Floors, roofs and ground take any size, and can be turned or tipped.
  - **White handles** size a drawn piece, each on its own stem: a wall's ends, top, bottom and face (its thickness), and a floor's sides, top and underside. **Pivot to corner** moves the piece's pivot to its low corner.
  - **Taller than its look:** a wall higher than its look's storey repeats the look's storeys or stretches it, as you choose.
- **Texture mapping:** the **Texture mapping** fold in a drawn piece's Wall, Floor or Ground section gives a surface a pattern of your own. Click the surface, then set the pattern's size, fit or count, its offset and turn, flip it, or align it to the world so neighbouring pieces line up. You can also drag the pattern on the surface: the blue dot slides it, the ring round it turns it and the white dot sizes it.
- **Align to grid, level and game pose,** in the piece panel's **Align** section (<kbd>L</kbd> for Align to grid):
  - **Grid** brings a piece onto the snap setting's steps.
  - **Level** puts a floor piece at a storey's height.
  - **Game pose** puts a ladder, door, car, van or fracturing piece at the nearest place the game's rules allow.
- **Diagonal cover:** **View options → Diagonal cover** outlines the map's tiles where a unit in the corner of a slanted wall gets cover along the diagonal (mostly at UFO hull corners). Amber tiles the Build keeps as the map has them. Blue tiles have something of your project on them, and the Build works them out again as the game's own build would, keeping their diagonal cover while the slanted wall stands. A slanted wall you copy or move gets diagonal cover at its new place.
- **Map settings…** (Map menu, or **View options → Sun, moon and sky…**): the map's sun, moon and sky, and its **background sound**. Keep the map's own sound, choose another of the game's 26 background sounds, or none. **Play** previews it in the editor, and the volume bar sets how loud it is.
- **Clear map takes the map's triggers out**, so a cleared map plays no leftover events: no wading where a stream was, and no narration over an empty field. Tick **Keep the map's triggers and scripts** to keep them.
- **The Wall section says how a wall plays:** a drawn wall standing inside a row of tiles, rather than on the lines between them, takes those tiles, and each of its doorways and windows says whether units walk through or the tile is blocked. A drawn floor says which tiles it is floor on.

### Changes
- **The Snap setting governs every move, size and turn.** With snap off, everything moves and sizes smoothly. With snap on, the steps count from where a drag started, so a piece off the grid keeps its offset, and **Tile** steps heights in levels of 64. Landing zones follow the snap too and keep their height.
- **Play in game installs the build under names of its own** (`<Map>_Play`). A play goes beside a map pack or a campaign install of the same map and leaves them alone, and Play's window says the name. Undoing a build in **Game maps…** takes its play out with it.
- **Baked builds are much smaller:** each copy of one of the map's own pieces gets lightmaps the size of what it shows, not a copy of its original's whole lightmap. A reported 340 MB map is now 56 MB, and the game uses less memory to play it.
- **Every map holds its baked lighting inside it.** No build, map pack or install ships a texture cache any more.
- **A Build that would pass Windows' path limit stops before it starts** and says how much too long the path is. A baked Build that relights nothing says "lighting unchanged".

### Bugs fixed
- **Built maps lost the diagonal cover at slanted walls** (the corners of the UFO maps' hulls) near any change, and across the whole map if its grid was grown.
- **Quicksaving could crash the game** on a built map with deleted pieces.
- **Terror civilians could start in the air** where a deleted piece, such as a UFO, had its floors.
- **A baked copy of the map's own floor showed black patches** where something stands on the original.
- **Play in game failed while a map pack of the same map was installed** in the game.
- **A Furies project failed to build** ("package is not compressed").
- **On most maps the editor took the map's own landing as moved** with nothing changed, and hid its Skyranger. A Build that changed landing zones then moved that landing's soldier points by up to four tiles.
- **The Build menu did nothing in the prefab builder.** It now draws walls, rooms, floors, roofs and ground there, and the prefab keeps them.
- **Shortcuts stopped working after a list, tick box or slider was used**, until the view was clicked.
- **An effect copy's panel pushed its number fields past its edge,** and **the Map grid's panel opened under the Selected piece panel**, hiding its Done button.
- **Saving or building could fail while another program had the file open for a moment** (an antivirus or a sync tool, say).
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - A damaged map-picture package in the game no longer stops the Project manager's list.
  - A prefab's Test in game now works with a long builds folder.
  - A Build now runs from a folder whose name has letters outside the system's code page.
  - A Replace build now takes out a map record typed by hand with spaces round `=`.
  - Reset all mends the map's broken pieces.
  - A prefab saved from a selection keeps its drawn and broken pieces.
  - Save as keeps the map picture.
  - A project still opens when one of its pieces can't be drawn.

### Known limits
- **A cover block grouped with a drawn wall keeps its cover when the wall is blown up.** Keep the block out of the wall's group, or add a breakable prop to the group beside the block: the block goes with the prop.
- **A drawn floor or roof turned or tipped:** the cursor may not land on it where it slopes or at its corners. A unit on one sloping more than about 10 degrees gets no cover, and past 45 degrees it is no floor. The Build warns about each such piece.
- **A roof or floor you break with Break takes no more damage in battle**, as the game keeps its own broken ones, unless you tick **Breaks further in battle** on it.
- **Tested mainly with Long War Rebalance through Steam.** Plain Enemy Within is meant to work but is less tested. Reports from it, or from any other setup, are welcome: use **Save a bug report** in the Build window.
