An early preview: see the README for what you need and the user guide (in the release, and online at <https://ruskyandrei.github.io/xcom-map-workshop/>) for how it works.

**Download** `XCOM-Map-Workshop-0.7.1-Setup.exe` (recommended) or the zip. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.

**Upgrading from 0.7.0:** install over it (Setup asks you to close a running editor). Your projects, prefabs, builds and the maps you installed in the game are kept.
- **Each map is read from the game again the first time you open it** (about half a minute), because the editor now reads which wall each diagonal cover mark belongs to and where the game reworks the map as a battle starts. **Cache everything…** (under **Settings**, the gear) with **Every map, ready to open** does them all at once.
- **A project or prefab saved in 0.7.1 can't be opened in 0.7.0.** If you pass one on, or a map pack with its project files, the other person needs 0.7.1 too.
- **Diagonal cover is now only where there is a mark.** The Build no longer works out diagonal cover by itself. The map's own marks stay, and go with their walls when you move or copy them. Anything else that got diagonal cover from the Build in 0.7.0, such as a drawn wall at a slant, needs a mark from the new Diagonal cover view (below).
- **Clear map's tick "Keep the map's triggers and scripts" is gone:** the window now has a tick for each part of the map, and the triggers are one of them. A project that cleared its map opens with the same parts cleared as before.
- **Under Long War, a cleared map no longer gets Long War's patch levels** for that map (their lights, scripts, no-spawn volumes and Meld points), from its next Build on.

### New features
- **The Diagonal cover view** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>, or the Views list): place diagonal cover by hand. A new mark comes from the Add panel's **Diagonal cover** entry. Click a mark to pick it, click its corner dots to switch each corner on or off, turn it with <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd>, move it by whole tiles with its gizmo, and set **Cover** to Full or Half.
  - A mark follows its wall: move, turn, copy or delete the wall and the mark goes with it. A piece brought from another map brings its marks, and so does a prefab.
  - A mark on a tile that the game works out again at the start of every battle shows a yellow warning triangle, and its panel says which area to move it off.
- **One Views button on the tool strip** lists every view (Normal, Buildings, Lights, Landing zones, Effects, Spawns, Diagonal cover, Map grid), each with its switches under its row.
- **Clear map by parts:** the Clear map window has a tick for each of six parts: pieces and ground, lights, effects and sounds, spawn points, landing zones, triggers and scripts. Clear only the lights, say, or everything but the landing zones. A part already cleared is marked, and the window says what a partial clear leaves behind.
- **The Open window and one Map button.** The header's second button names the map or prefab that is open, and its menu holds that map's or prefab's items and **Open**: **A map…** or **A prefab…** (<kbd>M</kbd>). The Open window has a Maps side and a Prefabs side, the prefabs as cards with pictures; the prefab library's files are managed there. The editor also has a new logo.
- **The Add panel made large:** its new maximise button spreads it over the view, with each tab's pieces as cards with large pictures.
- **This prefab,** a new tab of the Add panel in the prefab builder: one row for each distinct piece the prefab holds, with its count. A click places another, tint and flags with it.
- **The piece behind:** hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and turn the wheel to step through everything under the cursor, nearest first. It also picks one piece of a group on its own, without ungrouping, to move, copy or delete it alone.
- **A copy goes along to the next map or prefab:** <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>, open another map, project or prefab, then <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>. Pieces the other map doesn't have are brought as pieces from other maps are.
- **The same controls on everything:** landing zones, spawn points, diagonal cover marks and a building's floor boxes take a piece's keys and tool strip buttons (<kbd>G</kbd>, <kbd>R</kbd>, <kbd>V</kbd>, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+drag to copy, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>, <kbd>X</kbd> and <kbd>V</kbd>, <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd>).
- **A building's own cursor floors:** tick **Cursor stops at its floors** on a building in the Buildings view, and the game's cursor steps through that building's floors as its boxes lay them out. New buildings start with the tick on, and prefabs keep it.
- **Long War Rebalance map info** (Map settings, for Long War Rebalance 3 beta 17 and later): **Largest abduction** says the largest abduction the map suits, and **Keep Hold the Line off this map** does that. A map pack carries both.
- **Play in game beside another copy of XCOM:** if you have another copy of the game open, Play in game starts this install's game as a second one beside it.

### Changes
- **The Build takes deleted pieces out of the map's script.** A deleted piece the script only acts on is now a note, not a warning, so a cleared map's Build window is no longer full of red rows. Each script warning names its piece and says what happens.
- **Clear map takes more:** the project's added effects, ambient sounds and lens flares go with its added pieces, a baked Build of a map with its lights cleared lights the remaining pieces again without them, and the window gives one short count of what goes.
- **The Buildings view no longer lists the map's changed and deleted buildings.** A changed building's box still has **Restore the map's**; a deleted one comes back with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> or **Reset all**.
- **The Views list and the Build menu open beside their buttons,** and the Build menu no longer shows over the open Add panel.

### Bugs fixed
- **A door in a slanted UFO wall gave diagonal cover against its jambs** on a built map.
- **The Build's "Compile the edit" step took minutes** on a project with flat pieces much larger than the map, such as a sea round a boat: about two minutes became a second.
- **The Build window's rows about the map's script all read the same,** with the piece's name cut off.
- **Clear map left the project's added effects and sounds standing.**
- **A dialog taller than the screen** pushed its buttons out of view: it now scrolls under them.
- **A group's section in the piece panel had a gap** where a button was hidden.

### Known limits
- **A diagonal cover mark gives no cover on a tile the game works out again as the battle starts** (areas round some of Long War's changes to a map, and round mission objects). The view shows a yellow triangle on such a mark: move it off the area its panel names. A blast near a mark takes its diagonal cover away too, as it does on the game's own maps.
- **A cover block grouped with a drawn wall keeps its cover when the wall is blown up.** Keep the block out of the wall's group, or add a breakable prop to the group beside the block: the block goes with the prop.
- **A drawn floor or roof turned or tipped:** the cursor may not land on it where it slopes or at its corners. A unit on one sloping more than about 10 degrees gets no cover, and past 45 degrees it is no floor. The Build warns about each such piece.
- **Tested mainly with Long War Rebalance through Steam.** Plain Enemy Within is meant to work but is less tested. Reports from it, or from any other setup, are welcome: use **Save a bug report** in the Build window.
