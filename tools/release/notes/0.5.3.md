An early preview: see the README for what you need and the user guide (in the release, and online at <https://ruskyandrei.github.io/xcom-map-workshop/>) for how it works.

**Download** `XCOM-Map-Workshop-0.5.3-Setup.exe` (recommended) or the zip. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.

**Upgrading from 0.5.2:** install over it. Your projects, prefabs, builds and the maps you installed in the game are kept.
- **Each map is read from the game again the first time you open it** (about half a minute), because the editor now reads each piece's flags and the map's tile grid from the game's own record. **Cache everything…** with **Every map, ready to open** does them all at once. Pieces from other maps are read again too, the first time each is used.
- **A project or prefab saved in 0.5.3 can't be opened in 0.5.2.** If you pass one on, or a map pack with its project files, the other person needs 0.5.3 too.
- **Projects saved before keep the map names they had.** Only new projects are named after the project (below).

### New features
- **Piece flags:** the piece panel's new **Flags** group turns on or off five things the game reads on a piece: **Blocks walking**, **Gives cover**, **Blocks sight**, **Floor** and **Mouse picks it**. A wall units walk and see through, a sign the mouse never picks, a fence that gives no cover.
  - A click changes every piece you picked, in one undo step; copies, pasted pieces and prefabs keep the flags, and **Reset flags** puts the map's back. A **Flags** chip marks a piece with one off or changed.
  - The Build writes them on the piece and works its tiles out again. A project with only flags builds without relighting.
- **Select all of a kind**, beside Copy and Delete, selects every piece drawn with the same mesh, or only those inside a drawn box, so you can change them all at once.
- **Your own test squad:** **Settings** (the gear in the header), then **Test squad setup…**, chooses the squad every Play in game lands with, for maps, projects, Land here and prefab tests.
  - Under Long War Rebalance: one to eight soldiers, MEC troopers and SHIVs, each with class, rank, armour, weapons, items, perks and stats from your install's own lists. On plain Enemy Within and Long War without Rebalance: soldiers only, six at most.
  - Each change is checked at once: errors stop a play, warnings say what the game will do. **Export…** and **Import…** share a squad as a file. Until you save one, it is the six squaddies as before.
- **Build in the project manager** builds the ticked projects one after another, each a full build with its lighting baked, while you go on editing. A chip in the header shows how far it is, with **Stop**, **Resume** and **Clear**. If the editor closes mid-queue, the queue is kept, stopped, until you resume it.
- **The project manager, laid out again:** your projects on the left, each with its last build in one word (Ready, Not baked, Out of date, Not built) and filters with counts; the ticked ones on the right, each with its own **Additional** or **Replace** and its warnings under it.
  - **Export map pack** (was Zip as a map pack) can **Include editor project files**, so whoever gets the pack can open and change the maps.
- **Maps named after their project:** a new project builds as its own name, one word of up to 24 characters, shown under the name in Save and fixed once saved. Its baked lighting goes in `<Map>_Lighting.tfc`. A build or an install never replaces another project's files.
- **Maps up to 256 tiles a side** in Map grid (128 before).

### Changes
- **Each map's tile grid comes from the game's own record.** The Battleship, Deluge and Furies have one for the first time, so edits that need it (moving a van, a building's floors, Map grid) build there, and the Battleship has its 15 levels.
- **The user guide is online** at <https://ruskyandrei.github.io/xcom-map-workshop/>, as well as in the release. The online one can be ahead of the last release.

### Bugs fixed
- **A project on Roadhouse that moved a van failed to build** ("has no tile grid, which a van needs").
- **A map pack or install replacing Commercial Alley Redux left a new campaign unable to start its first mission.** Your map now keeps the record name the first mission asks for. A map installed or packed this way with 0.5.2 needs installing or exporting again.
- **Replace took the original map away from missions your map isn't for.** Now the original keeps the missions your project leaves out, and the warnings under Replace name them.
- **Map grid snapped back** when dragged larger on a map already over 128 tiles a side, such as the Temple Ship.
- **An invisible floor placed with the snap off or fine** could stand above or below the ground where the ground isn't flat.

### Known limits
- **Blocks walking on a low piece** (under 60 units over the bottom of its level, such as a hedge or a kerb) stops no one: the panel and the Build say so. A door with Blocks walking or Blocks sight off is walked or seen through even when shut.
- **A test battle is not a campaign mission:** your test squad, four pods of Sectoids or EXALT, and no mission commander.
- **A new map starts from one of the game's, and only the game's own pieces can be used.** Clear map empties it, but the sun, the time of day and the base missions stay the base map's. You can't import your own models, textures or sounds.
- **Tested mainly with Long War Rebalance through Steam.** Plain Enemy Within is meant to work but is less tested. Reports from it, or from any other setup, are welcome: use **Save a bug report** in the Build window.
