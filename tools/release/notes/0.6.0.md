An early preview: see the README for what you need and the user guide (in the release, and online at <https://ruskyandrei.github.io/xcom-map-workshop/>) for how it works.

**Download** `XCOM-Map-Workshop-0.6.0-Setup.exe` (recommended) or the zip. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.

**Upgrading from 0.5.3:** install over it. Your projects, prefabs, builds and the maps you installed in the game are kept.
- **Each map is read from the game again the first time you open it** (about half a minute), because the editor now reads the map's effects, sounds and spawn points. **Cache everything…** (now under **Settings**, the gear) with **Every map, ready to open** does them all at once.
- **A project saved in 0.6.0 can't be opened in 0.5.3**, and neither can a prefab that holds effects, sounds or drawn pieces. If you pass one on, or a map pack with its project files, the other person needs 0.6.0 too.
- **The header changed** (below): Build and Play are buttons now, and several items moved out of the Project menu.

### New features
- **Draw your own walls, rooms, floors, roofs and ground.** Hover **Add** on the tool strip for the new **Build** menu, pick a tool and drag it out in the view.
  - **Walls and rooms:** a wall of any length and height, with doorways and windows you add and drag by their handles. **Room** draws four joined walls with a doorway; **Room with a roof** adds the roof and a building around it, so the roof is cut away when the cursor is inside.
  - **Floors and roofs** of any size, a storey over where you press. They break in the game a piece at a time, as the game's own do.
  - **Ground:** a flat **Patch** to cover holes and paint the ground, a raised **Block**, a **Ramp**, and a **Rock** shaped by dragging its blue points.
  - **Looks:** a drawn piece starts plain. **Choose…** in the piece panel (or <kbd>I</kbd>) opens the look picker, with the look of every wall, floor, roof and ground of your game's maps; the eyedropper takes a look from a piece in the view.
  - **Break and Repair:** knock fragments out of a drawn wall, floor or roof, or out of one of the map's own breakable pieces, so the map starts the battle with the damage.
- **Effects and sounds:** the new **Effects view** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>X</kbd>) shows the map's fires, smoke, steam, ambient sounds and lens flares. Move, copy and delete them, change an effect's size and particle settings and a sound's reach, volume and pitch, and add any effect or sound from the game's other maps. Effects are drawn in the view as a likeness of what the game shows. Prefabs keep theirs.
- **Spawn points:** the new **Spawns view** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>U</kbd>) shows where the aliens' pods start, the Meld, the drop-in points and the mission's objectives. Move and copy pods and Meld points, move drop-in points, add new pods, Meld points and no-spawn volumes from the Add panel, and move an added bomb mission's objects. Under Long War, **Scripted drop-ins land where placed** keeps the mission's reinforcements at their own points.
- **Map picture…** (Map menu): the picture the game shows for your map on its mission screens. Choose an image and frame it, or use **Take from the view**; the Build writes it into the map. Long War Rebalance 3 shows a map's own picture.
- **Map packs for Long War Rebalance 3:** **Export map pack** in the project manager makes a drop-in pack: one folder copied into the game's `CookedPCConsole`, read by LWR 3.00.00 beta 15 or later, with no file of the game changed; delete the folder and it is gone. The pack's `Install.cmd` and `Uninstall.cmd` do the copying, and install the classic way on other installs. The editor remembers your packs, so exporting one again makes its next version.
- **Add to group** puts the selected pieces into a group that is already there.

### Changes
- **The header is sorted out.** The Project menu held fifteen items; now:
  - **Project:** Save, Save as…, Open… (was Load…), Close project, your recent projects and Manage projects….
  - **Map** is a menu: Change map… (<kbd>M</kbd>), Missions…, Map picture…, Map grid and Clear map….
  - **Build** and **Play** are buttons at the right of the header. The arrow joined to Play has Test squad setup…, Game maps… and the installs the editor works with.
  - **Settings** (the gear): Game install and checks… and Cache everything….
  - **Find a piece** is gone, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> is your browser's own again.
- **The piece panel is tidier:** icon buttons under the name, the piece's own settings first, and Group, Align, Flags, Place and Source each folding to one line. A section's ⓘ shows its how-to text.
- **A baked build keeps its lighting inside the map file.** No `.tfc` file is built, installed or packed with it.
- **Clear map** also deletes the map's pods, Meld points, drop-in points and ambient sounds, and counts them in its window. The Skyranger and its landing stay.
- **The user guide is in a new order:** getting started and typical tasks first, then the reference in four parts.

### Bugs fixed
- **A project on the small scout's farm map failed to build or play** ("no DefaultMaps.ini record for CsmallScout_Farm"): the map's file and the game's records spell its name in different case.
- **A data recovery or covert extraction played with a SHIV first in the test squad never left its loading screen.** The squad's first soldier or MEC trooper now goes to the front, and the play's notes say so.

### Known limits
- **A roof or floor you break with Break takes no more damage in battle**, as the game keeps its own broken ones, unless you tick **Breaks further in battle** on it.
- **The look picker draws its pictures the first time you open it**, which takes a few minutes; after that they are kept.
- **A drop-in map pack needs Long War Rebalance 3.00.00 beta 15 or later.** On older versions and on plain Enemy Within, a pack installs the classic way, by `Install.cmd`.
- **Tested mainly with Long War Rebalance through Steam.** Plain Enemy Within is meant to work but is less tested. Reports from it, or from any other setup, are welcome: use **Save a bug report** in the Build window.
