An early preview: see the README for what you need and the user guide (in the release) for how it works.

**Download** `XCOM-Map-Workshop-0.4.0-Setup.exe` (recommended) or the zip. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.

**Upgrading from 0.3.x:** install over it. Your projects and builds are kept. Each map you prepared before re-exports by itself the first time you open it ("Map schema updated. Re-exporting.", 10 to 30 seconds), so that the fixes below show. Some keyboard shortcuts have moved (see Changes).

### New features
- **Invisible pieces** in the Add panel (kind **Invisible**). The editor builds them itself, so they work on any install, with no Long War files needed.
  - **Cover block**: an invisible box that gives high or low cover and blocks movement. You can set its width and length (1 to 16 tiles each way) and its height.
  - **Invisible floor**: thin plates, one per tile, that soldiers can stand on, ignored for cover. Use one to make a walkable spot where the game finds no floor. It is placed on the ground. Its panel shows how high it stands over the ground at each tile, and **Fit to ground** puts it just over the ground as the game sees it (its collision, not the drawn mesh).
- **Basic Training** (Act1_IntroLevel, the tutorial's map) is in the map browser under Abduction. You can edit it, build it and play it.
- **Camera keys**:
  - **WASD** moves the camera like the arrow keys, and **Q** and **E** turn the view.
  - View options has a new **Keys move along the view** setting. With it on, forward and back follow the way the camera faces, and a left-drag looks round from where the camera stands instead of orbiting.
- **Play area**: the map's playable bounds show as faint amber walls. **B**, or View options' **Play area**, hides and shows them.
- **Play in game in the build window**: once a build finishes, its window has a Play in game button that installs the build and starts the battle on it.
- **Maps update themselves**: a map prepared by an earlier version re-exports when you open it.

### Changes
- **Shortcuts moved** to make room for WASD and Q/E:
  - Move is **G** (was W).
  - Rotate is **R** (was E). While placing a piece, R still turns it.
  - The Add panel's piece list is **P** (was A).
  - Solo is **O** (was S).
  - World or local axes is **X** (was Q).
- **One camera speed:** the camera keys move at the same speed on every map and at every zoom, about 52 tiles a second (Shift for three times that). It used to follow the zoom, so it was very fast on big maps.
- **No snapping limits:** with snap off, any piece moves and turns freely, including ladders, doors, cars, vans and fracturing walls and floors. An **Off the grid** chip warns when such a piece is off its grid. Tile snap still keeps them on it.
- **Wheel zoom:** the mouse wheel zooms towards what is under the cursor.
- **Interior** draws a piece that reaches into the area whole, instead of slicing it off at the edge (a car across the edge now shows entire).

### Bugs fixed
- Some props drew as see-through glass or with holes: the Farm's tractors, dairy tanks and pipes, and a barn lamp. Masked materials now use their real cut-out mask (maps re-export to pick this up).
- Zooming in barely moved on big maps once you were zoomed in away from the map's centre.
- On some maps, a full build stopped with "Texture has no own 8-byte TextureFileCacheName" (seen on Street Overpass with copied pieces).
- A full build of DLC1_1_LowFriends stopped while relighting (a tester's bug report: thank you).
- Variants of the alien bases (AlienBase01, AlienBase02) stuck in the entrance cutscene, so the battle never started. AlienBase02 hasn't been played since the fix.
- The getting-started notes' line on plain Enemy Within no longer mentions the old first-mission install.

### Known limits
- Tried with Long War Rebalance through Steam. Plain Enemy Within, other Long War versions and game folders outside Steam haven't been tried.
- The test battle is a plain one with six squaddies, not a campaign mission.
- A cover block gives no cover to a soldier standing on a slope (the game's rule), though it still blocks movement there. Ragdolls and debris pass through blocks.
- Pieces moved off the grid build with a warning, but haven't been played in the game yet. A ladder off the grid climbs from the tiles its ends fall in.
