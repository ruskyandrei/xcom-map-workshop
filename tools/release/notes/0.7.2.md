An early preview: see the README for what you need and the user guide (in the release, and online at <https://ruskyandrei.github.io/xcom-map-workshop/>) for how it works.

**Download** `XCOM-Map-Workshop-0.7.2-Setup.exe` (recommended) or the zip. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.

**Upgrading from 0.7.1:** install over it (Setup asks you to close a running editor). Your projects, prefabs, builds and the maps you installed in the game are kept.
- **Nothing is read from the game again:** maps open as they did, and the pieces of other maps already in the editor's cache are kept.
- **Projects and prefabs are saved as in 0.7.1,** so 0.7.1 still opens what 0.7.2 saves.
- **A building you made in the editor is fixed in the game once its map is built again** (see Bugs fixed). A map built with 0.7.1 or before keeps the old shapes until then.

### New features
- **Cache everything draws every picture too:** **Cache everything…** (under **Settings**, the gear) has a third option, **Every look's and piece's picture**, ticked by default. It draws every look's sample in the look picker and every piece's and effect's picture in the Add panel's All maps, so none of them says *Drawing…* the first time it shows. The editor's page draws them itself once the pieces are read, so keep it open until they are done (a hidden browser tab waits). A second progress bar follows them, and **Cancel** and **Carry on** work as they do for the pieces.

### Changes
- **A new editor version no longer reads every piece of other maps again.** The pieces in the cache are read again only when what a piece holds changes, not with every change to how maps are read, which saved minutes of reading and gigabytes of writing at each upgrade.

### Bugs fixed
- **A building made in the editor was cut down to its first floor in the game** with the cursor on some of its tiles, on about a third of the maps (among them the bar, the police station, the truck stop, the Abductor maps, the Chryssalid hive and the train yard). On a boat made on the hive map, the mouse also dropped the cursor into the hold. The Build now gives a made building's volumes the shape of its boxes. **Build the map again** to get the fix.
- **A prefab saved by 0.7.0 or earlier couldn't be placed:** the Prefabs tab said the page was older than the editor's server and to reload it, which didn't help. Such a prefab is placed as it is now.

### Known limits
- **A diagonal cover mark gives no cover on a tile the game works out again as the battle starts** (areas round some of Long War's changes to a map, and round mission objects). The view shows a yellow triangle on such a mark: move it off the area its panel names. A blast near a mark takes its diagonal cover away too, as it does on the game's own maps.
- **A blended ground look shows one of its textures in the editor:** the forest and terrain grounds, river banks and vertex-painted asphalt mix several textures in the game, but the editor draws only one, often not the one the game shows. A made ground in Rocks River Vert Paint is rock in the editor and green grass in the game. Use Play in game to see the look as it is.
- **A cover block grouped with a drawn wall keeps its cover when the wall is blown up.** Keep the block out of the wall's group, or add a breakable prop to the group beside the block: the block goes with the prop.
- **Tested mainly with Long War Rebalance through Steam.** Plain Enemy Within is meant to work but is less tested. Reports from it, or from any other setup, are welcome: use **Save a bug report** in the Build window.
