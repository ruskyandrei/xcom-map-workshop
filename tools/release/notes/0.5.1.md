An early preview: see the README for what you need and the user guide (in the release) for how it works.

**Download** `XCOM-Map-Workshop-0.5.1-Setup.exe` (recommended) or the zip. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.

**Upgrading from 0.5.0:** install over it. Your projects, prefabs, builds and the maps you installed in the game are kept, and no map has to be read from the game again.
- **A prefab or project taller than 18 levels, or a building with more than 8 floors, can't be opened in 0.5.0.** If you pass one on, the other person needs 0.5.1 too.

### New features
- **Cache everything…** (Project menu) reads every piece of the game's maps into the editor's cache now, so placing a piece of another map or opening a prefab never waits for it. Tick **Every map, ready to open** to prepare every map as well.
  - The window says how many there are, about how long it takes and how much disk space it needs (here about 10 minutes and 6.4 GB for the pieces of 116 maps), with the drive's free space.
  - It runs in the background with a progress bar: keep working, close the window and open it again later. **Cancel** stops it at any moment, what was read so far stays, and **Carry on** picks up from there.
- **30 levels.** Maps (Map grid), prefabs and the prefab test's stage go up to 30 levels high (1,920 units), where 18 was the limit. The cursor gets up to ten storeys and a building up to ten floors. Over 18 levels, the height of the game's tallest maps, the editor says "not played".
- **The prefab builder's Problems chip**, under the size line: what **Test in game** would say of the prefab as it stands, without building it (how many pieces would be left out, how many warnings). A row selects and frames its piece. It is checked when you open and save the prefab, and by **Check again**.

### Changes
- **More is built.** Where the Build or the prefab test used to stop, it now builds what it can and says what it couldn't:
  - **A piece the Build can't make is left out**, and the rest of the map is built. The Build window lists the pieces left out, and a row selects its piece. A project whose every edit is left out says so.
  - **Pieces the editor hadn't let through are built with a warning:** pieces of a kind the Build hasn't made before (marked **Untried**), a single part of a car or a door edited on its own, a tinted copy (built without its tint), and pieces of other maps the library used to hold back. If one of them makes the Build fail, they are taken out, the rest is built, and the window names them.
  - **Light values out of range** are built at the nearest value in range, with a warning. A piece edited whose glow can't follow it is built, with a warning, and the glow stays where it was.
- **The prefab test follows the Build's rules.**
  - A piece off the grid is a warning, not an error, and a rotation rounded to what the game stores no longer stops it.
  - A piece whose source can't be found or read is left out and named, and the test waits for **Launch test** so you can decide.
  - When the map a prefab's pieces came from has changed since the prefab was made (a game update, another install), its pieces are looked up by what they are, with a warning.
  - The stage is as tall as the prefab needs, 18 to 30 levels.
- **Prefabs from elsewhere open more often:**
  - a prefab larger than a map holds is saved and placed, with a warning;
  - one placed where it reaches out of the map's grid is placed, with a notice;
  - a field this editor doesn't know is kept, and named on the prefab's card.
- **Warnings only for what can cause a problem in the game.** What can't (loot counts, how pieces will look, the cutaway's details, unchanged edits) is no longer listed among the warnings, and the prefab test lists warnings only. A piece sunk into the ground no longer counts as outside the grid.
- **A building's floors:** which pieces belong to a floor of a building you draw or change now skips pieces that collide with nothing (lamps, wires, decoration), which matches how the game's own maps are listed more closely.
- **A faster editor**, measured on Forest Grove with a 460-piece Landed Large UFO prefab:
  - opening the prefab in the builder: about a second, from 20 seconds;
  - checking it: just over a second, from 16;
  - each edit in the builder: 9–15 ms, from 50–85;
  - pasting 500 pieces: under a tenth of a second, from 1.3 s;
  - drawing the map: the sun's shadows are drawn again when something changes (and once a second), not every frame.
- **A visible busy state:** opening a prefab or a project counts on the loading screen, placing and pasting count the pieces, and deleting, undoing or redoing 60 pieces or more shows a busy mark with a bar.
- The Build panel and the guide give a baked build's time as about a minute or two for a small edit (they still said several minutes).

### Bugs fixed
- **The Small Landed UFO prefab showed its roof on floor 1.** Some of the game's maps list which pieces belong to which floor by hand (the UFO's roof is one), and those lists were lost as soon as the building's floor boxes were moved or changed, in a prefab or in a project. They are carried through such edits now.
- **A prefab with spot lights copied from a map failed its test** with "outerConeAngle must be 0 to 89.99". Some maps store a spot light's cone wider than the game uses (the UFO maps' blue lights store 100). The editor now stores the cone the game lights it as (89.99), so it looks the same. A prefab saved before tests as it is, and is corrected the next time it is saved.
- **Copies of some pieces were refused** because their mesh sits off the piece's own centre (a fence in the Landed Large UFO). Such a copy now keeps the offset and is built.

### Known limits
- **Not played in the game yet:** what 0.5.1 adds (maps and prefabs over 18 levels, builds with pieces left out, pieces built with the Untried warning) and the faster bake's lighting from 0.5.0. They build without errors; please report how they play, with **Save a bug report** from the Build panel.
- **A new map starts from one of the game's.** Clear map empties it, but the sun, the time of day and the mission stay the base map's.
- **Only the game's own pieces:** you can't import your own models, textures or sounds.
- **Tried with Long War Rebalance through Steam.** Plain Enemy Within is a best guess, and other Long War versions and game folders outside Steam haven't been tried.
