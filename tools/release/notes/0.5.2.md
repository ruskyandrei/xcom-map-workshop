An early preview: see the README for what you need and the user guide (in the release) for how it works.

**Download** `XCOM-Map-Workshop-0.5.2-Setup.exe` (recommended) or the zip. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.

**Upgrading from 0.5.1:** install over it. Your projects, prefabs, builds and the maps you installed in the game are kept.
- **Each map is read from the game again the first time you open it** (about half a minute), because the editor now reads each mission's objects. **Cache everything…** with **Every map, ready to open** does them all at once.
- **A project or prefab with cover blocks, saved in 0.5.2, can't be opened in 0.5.1:** blocks are now stored by their size in units. If you pass one on, the other person needs 0.5.2 too.

### New features
- **Play in game plays the mission you pick**, not always an abduction: the header's **Mission** picker, or the play window's **Mission** select, chooses it. Under the choice a line says how far that kind is known to work played this way.
  - Played and working under Long War Rebalance: abduction, council bomb, council rescue, terror site, crashed UFO, base defence, the alien base and data recovery.
  - The enemies are four pods of Sectoids (five on a terror site). EXALT's missions get four pods of EXALT operatives, each with a heavy or a sniper.
- **Missions…** (Project menu) chooses which of the base map's missions your project is for, and can give a map more through its record: an abduction, a terror site, a council bomb (Long War) or a crashed or landed UFO (on a map with a UFO building). **Clear map** then asks **What is this map for?**
- **A mission's objects in the view:** View options' **Mission objects** marks what the picked mission adds: drop-in points, pods, your soldiers' points, the operative, VIPs, capture points, radar arrays, the bomb and its power nodes. A click says what each is and what it moves with.
  - **Move one with its group** by its arrows, in whole tiles and levels. The Build moves it in a copy of that mission's files.
  - **The Build warns** when an edit takes the floor from under a mission's objects, and the Build window lists them by mission.
- **Enable saving:** tick it in the play window, in **Build and test**, or in the tab on a finished build's **Play in game**. XCOM then opens at its main menu, **F5** there starts the battle, and you can save and load in it. It is always on for EXALT's missions under Long War, which saves and reloads them as they start.
- **Cover blocks of any size:** drag the white dots on a block's faces. The steps follow the snap setting (off, 24 units, whole tiles), and the top and bottom move too. The panel says what the game makes of a block smaller than a tile, and what the block does to line of sight. A thin block on a tile's edge acts as an invisible wall (played).
- **Blockers:** a cover block with **Cover: None (blocks walking)** stops units walking there without giving cover. It is drawn purple.
- **Camera speeds:** View options' Camera section has sliders for Move speed, Key turn speed and Drag turn speed. They are remembered, and a double-click puts one back.
- **Pivot** (tool strip, **C**): move and turn about the selection's centre instead of each piece's own origin.

### Changes
- **The prefab builder's Test in game** has **Bake the lighting** off by default, like the map's Build.
- **A build keeps the names the game looks for** in its missions: a bomb mission's record keeps `_Bomb`, so Long War still sends its reinforcements as for a bomb, and the Temple Ship's keeps `TempleShip`. A map the game knows by its whole name gets a note.
- **Text in the page no longer gets selected** when you drag. Piece details, logs and messages still can be.
- The editor now says **the original map** for the map from the game's files with none of your edits.

### Bugs fixed
- **Deluge (Progeny 2) couldn't be opened** ("Could not open scene: Invalid array length"): one of its lights reaches millions of units out.
- **Street Hurricane Terror's dropship landed in the middle of the map.** The game's own intro gives it no place; a build now lands it at the map's grounded Skyranger. The original map, played as the game has it, keeps the fault.
- **A terror play hung at the loading screen.** The civilians now get their looks.
- **Shift didn't speed up a camera move already under way.**

### Known limits
- **Not played in the game yet:** missions added through **Missions…**, moved mission objects, the other mission kinds (landed UFO, council assault and extraction, covert extraction, the EXALT raid, the final and story missions), and some of 0.5.1's additions (maps and prefabs over 18 levels, builds with pieces left out). They build without errors; please report how they play, with **Save a bug report** from the Build window.
- **A test battle is not a campaign mission:** a fixed squad of six, Sectoid or EXALT pods, and no mission commander.
- **A new map starts from one of the game's, and only the game's own pieces can be used.** Clear map empties it, but the sun, the time of day and the base missions stay the base map's. You can't import your own models, textures or sounds.
- **Tried with Long War Rebalance through Steam.** Plain Enemy Within is a best guess, and other Long War versions and game folders outside Steam haven't been tried.
