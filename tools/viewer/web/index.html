V · G · R
Select · Move · Rotate
T
Box select: drag a box over the ground, stretch it by the white dots, move it by the dot in its middle. Enter selects every piece whose middle is inside, Shift+Enter adds them to the selection, Esc puts the box away. In the Lights view it takes lights. With the Normal button's switches on it takes lights and buildings with the pieces
P
Add: the pieces this map has (Kind: Floors for its floor pieces), and new lights (Kind: Lights). Click one, then click in the view to place it; Shift+click places more. A floor piece is placed as any piece; Align to level puts it on its levels
X
World or the piece's own axes
C
Pivot: move and turn about the piece's own origin (usually a corner), or about the middle of the selection, so a piece turns in place. With tile snap the middle keeps to a tile's centre or corner, so pieces stay on the tiles
Drag a white dot
On a selected cover block or invisible floor: move that face (its sides, a block's top and bottom) to size it, in the snap setting's steps (off: smoothly; 24: steps of 24; 96: whole tiles across, levels of 64 up). A low side's drag moves its corner, so the far side stays. Esc during the drag puts it back
Drag a white dot
On a selected made wall, floor, roof or ground shape: size it by that face (a wall's ends, top, bottom and the face toward you for its thickness; a floor's sides, top and underside), in the snap setting's steps; the opposite face and the piece's pivot stay. Click a gap's blue middle to show its own handles
Texture mapping
With a made wall's, floor's, roof's or ground shape's Texture mapping section open: click a surface of it to pick it (Tab: the next), then drag the blue dot to slide its pattern, the ring to turn it and the white dot to size a repeat, in the snap setting's steps; its size handles step aside meanwhile. Esc during the drag puts it back
J
Held while dragging a made wall's end: the end goes to the nearest other wall's end, where they meet in a mitred corner
L
Align to grid: the selection's pivot onto the nearest step of the snap setting (with snap off, the nearest tile corner and level), turned to whole angular steps

Lights

Shift+L
The Lights view (the rail's bulb): click a marker to select it, then move, turn, recolour, copy or delete it as a piece. The light panel sets its colour, brightness, radius, falloff and cones; View options can outline what it is baked into or reaches
Drag a dot
On the selected light: the white dot sets its radius, a spot light's rim dots its outer cone (left) and inner cone (right). Esc during the drag puts it back
O
Solo: the map dimmed and the selected lights alone
P
Add: Point Light and Spot Light in the list (Kind: Lights), placed as pieces are

Landing zones, spawn points and objectives

Shift+Z
The Landing zones view (the rail's ⇧Z button): the zones drawn over everything, and only they are picked. A picked zone: G and R its arrows or ring, [ and ] turn it a quarter, Ctrl+D copies it, Delete deletes it, Esc lets it go. New ones come from the Add panel (Kind: Landing zones)
Shift+U
The Spawns view (the rail's pin): the map's pods (rings), Meld points, drop-in points (arrows), starts and other points, and the objectives of the mission picked in the header (gems), drawn over everything. Click one to see its values and where each comes from, its group, what uses it, what it uses and what the game does with it; a landing zone to see what it is. Hover the pin to choose which kinds show
Move one
A mission's objective or a stream's point: drag its arrows to move it with its group by whole tiles and levels. The Normal button's flyout's Spawn points and objectives draws and picks them with the pieces
Delete · Ctrl+D · [ ]
On a picked pod, Meld point or drop-in point of the map, or a copy of one: delete it, copy it, and turn a drop-in point a quarter
Esc
Let the picked one go

Effects and sounds

Shift+X
The Effects view (the rail's spark): the map's effects (sparks), sounds (speakers) and lens flares (stars), and those of the mission picked in the header, drawn over everything. Hover one for its name; click one to see what it plays, whether it starts with the mission, what of the mission's script names it and which floor hides it. A picked effect shows its system's fixed box, a picked sound where it is heard. Hover the spark to choose which kinds show
Edit one
Where the panel says the gizmo moves it: a picked effect or sound moves, turns, copies (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V) and deletes as a piece does, groups with pieces (Ctrl+G) and goes back with Reset. One a piece carries moves with it until moved by itself; its panel's Keep where it is leaves it where the map has it. The Normal button's flyout's Effects and sounds draws and selects them with the pieces
Esc
In the Effects view: let the picked one go

Gizmo axes: red is game X, blue is game Y, green is up (game Z). Edits are a trial until saved as a project (Ctrl+S); Build (Ctrl+B) saves it and makes its variant package, ready to install. Its panel's Bake the lighting is off by default: the build takes seconds and the edit's lighting is not right in the game, but its pathing, cover and line of sight are, for checking those first; tick it for the proper lighting, which takes minutes. Play (the header) starts XCOM straight in a battle on the open map, or on the project's build, with a test squad: pick Enemy Within if the game's launcher asks, and the battle starts by itself, with no campaign to start. With a project open it opens Build's panel first, to build it (Bake the lighting off by default) and play it once built, or to play its last build as it is when that is of what is on screen; the build is installed in your game under its own name, nothing of the game's own is changed, and it is taken out again when the game closes. Manage projects (Project menu) installs builds for campaigns, as additional maps or in place of the originals, and Game maps (the arrow beside Play) shows what the game uses and undoes any of it.

What builds: moving, rotating, deleting and copying scenery, props, breakable props (windows among them), collectibles, ladders, framed and frameless doors, cars, vans, fracturing walls and floors, physics props and lights. Ladders, doors and fracturing pieces use whole tiles and quarter turns. Pieces awaiting a build recipe carry a milestone in the inspector. A mission's own pieces and LWR's patches show for reference and can't be edited.
