### EXP-90's project: made pieces in the free form for the game runs

[exp90_projects.py](../../tools/editor/examples/exp90_projects.py) makes the project of
[EXP-90](../../research/experiments/EXP-90-free-form-pieces.md), the game runs of the custom geometry improvements
plan (section 7): on URB_Bar's car park, its cars, vans and wheel stops deleted, eleven made pieces in the free form
(fracWall 5, fracSlab 4): a wall off the tile lines at a fractional place and length with a window and a doorway, a
fractional upper floor against it with a hole across a tile edge, two walls at a free angle (30 and 120 degrees) meeting
in a mitred corner, walls 260 and 600 tall (repeat and stretch) and one 48 thick with a doorway, an upper floor turned
20 degrees, and two walls of one look, one with its faces mapped (64 by 64, turned 30 degrees). It prints the map's own
pieces left on the groups' ground, the compile's warnings and notes and each made copy's numbers (a mitre's ends), and
builds with `--build` (baked unless `--quick`).

Checked: it compiles with no error and nothing left out (warnings: two doorways with no door, two floors outdoors, the
turned floor's `slab-turned`); the baked Build made all twelve pieces (the 600-tall repeat wall as two stacked) and
relit them, and the package read back holds each with its own mesh, collision and lightmap (the EXP note).
