### Known bug KB-8 taken out: a made wall at a free angle breaks where it is hit

The fix for KB-8 (a turned made wall's fracture columns along its own line, `frac_walls.turned_columns`; finding
F-145) was played in EXP-90 run 2 on 22 November 2026: the same wall at 30 degrees, hit by a grenade at run 1's point,
broke its lower rows from its start, its corner part standing to the pavement beside the wall it is mitred to (the
fragments that broke in run 1), and only its top band standing over the gap from its start, as the game breaks any
wall. [KNOWN-BUGS.md](../../KNOWN-BUGS.md) no longer lists it.

[EXP-90](../../research/experiments/EXP-90-free-form-pieces.md) run 2 and F-145 in the
