### A Build with baking makes a turned sun's shadows again for the whole map

The game-likeness plan's stage T-S4 (the sun plan's section 5). When the native edit's `sun` says the direction
changed (`directionChanged`), a Build with baking passes the copy tool `--sun-turned`
([build.py](../../tools/editor/build.py) `light`, beside cover blocks, flags or effects that alone relight nothing too),
the copy tool hands it to the relight ([native_copy.py](../../tools/bake/native_copy.py) `relight_command`), and the
relight, reading the new direction from the map the Build's native step turned it in, makes in its one write:

- every distance-field shadow map of the sun on a piece the level lists, traced from nothing in the scene after the
  edit and written as it is, in place of the reached ones an edit changes; the copies, the pieces of other maps, the
  made pieces and the debris volume's pieces included ([sun_bake.py](../../tools/bake/sun_bake.py) `whole_map_jobs`,
  on the relight's workers; [shadowmaps.py](../../tools/bake/shadowmaps.py) reads a debris piece's shadow maps, and
  `dominant_receivers` takes them only when asked, so an edit's relight leaves them as before);
- the sun's own map of the level for moving things, made as Lightmass makes one: its frame from the light's
  direction, its box from the map's importance volume, each depth the deepest of 5 x 5 rays across the texel, at the
  size the map has (`level_map`, `map_edits`; the places of its members are
  [lighting_records.py](../../tools/upk/lighting_records.py)'s `shadow_info_places`);
- each lighting sample's mark for the sun not reaching it, by one ray (`sample_marks`, `lighting_records.mark_edits`).

The last two are fixed-size edits that [lightmap_writer.py](../../tools/bake/lightmap_writer.py) `run` now takes beside
its operations (`edits`), written through `package_edit.write_edited` and read back (`sun_bake.verify`). The relight's
record says what was made (`sun`), and the Build's light stage carries it as `sunTurned`. A relit piece kept for the
next build ([relight.py](../../tools/bake/relight.py) `PieceCache`) is kept for one sun: its direction joined the key.
A full Build whose only change is a turned sun now bakes; one that keeps the direction, and every quick Build, still
does not.

Checked: the bake suite's new [test_sun_bake.py](../../tools/bake/tests/test_sun_bake.py) (the frame, box, depths and
marks on made-up geometry and records; URB_Bar's own moon given back as the map stores it; URB_Bar built through the
copy tool with the sun lane turning its moon to a sun 16 degrees up from the west, read back), the upk suite's
`shadow_info_places` on a made-up package and on URB_Bar, the copy tool's command line and the Build's wiring on the
real `sun` lane (the time-of-day test now expects the full Build to bake). On URB_Bar: 708 shadow maps traced in 28.9
s on 16 workers (the copy tool's whole run 31 s), their texels on the side a ray gives on 99.58% (99.99% away from the
edges), the samples' marks 222 to 455, each as its ray gives it. The rules against the install's maps, and the
readback, are [audit_suns.py](../../tools/upk/audit_suns.py)'s new `level-rule` and `turn`, in
[the evidence file](../../research/evidence/sun-audit-2026-11-27.json); F-195 to F-198.
[build_outputs.py](../../tools/editor/tests/build_outputs.py)' thirteen inputs recorded on the branch's base and on
the change (by name: the whole record refuses on the base, the lanes kismetCuts and streamHides being in no input):
twelve Builds the same byte for byte, their manifests naming the new module among the tools; bar-sun's baked Build now
hands the copy tool --bake --sun-turned (its recorder takes --sun-turned as one of the relight's options).

Known gaps: a piece with the sun on its IrrelevantLights has no shadow map to take the new sun and stays without it
(F-198: on URB_Bar 50 pieces the turned sun would reach); the lightmaps keep the old sun's bounce and the lighting
samples their old light, as the plan has it; the map of the level keeps its size, so its texels stretch to the new
box; nothing is seen in the game yet (EXP-100).
