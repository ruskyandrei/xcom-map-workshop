### Ctrl and the wheel step through what is under the cursor, a piece of a group picked on its own

Discord suggestion S16: a way to pick the piece behind the one in front. With the real Ctrl key held and the pointer
over the view, each notch of the wheel selects the next thing a click at that point could pick, nearest first, instead
of zooming.

- **The stepping** ([pick_step.js](../../tools/viewer/web/pick_step.js), new, `createPickStep`; its pure part
  [pick_stack.js](../../tools/viewer/web/pick_stack.js), new): wheel down the next further, up the next nearer, round at
  either end. It starts from what is selected when that is one of the things under the cursor (after a click took the
  front piece, one notch takes the one behind it), else the first notch down takes the nearest and up the furthest; a
  group picked whole with pieces under the cursor gives the nearest of them. The list is taken at the first notch of a
  hold of Ctrl and kept while the pointer stays within 4 pixels and the camera still. Each notch selects; the piece
  panel is filled once the wheel has rested 150 ms (selection.js `selectStepped` at each notch, `select` after), so ten
  quick notches fill it once. The hint line: "2 of 5 under the cursor: Desk_A · wheel for the next · with Shift it is
  added". With Shift as well, what was selected stays and the thing stopped on is added (what the selection holds
  already is passed over). A touchpad's pinch (a wheel event with ctrlKey and no key down) still zooms: the stepping
  asks for the Ctrl key itself, from its key events, cleared when the window loses focus.
  [navigation.js](../../tools/viewer/web/navigation.js)'s wheel asks it first.
- **What is stepped through**, the view that is on: in the normal view every piece along the ray (scene_pieces.js
  `pickAll`, the visible and unclipped hits `pick` takes the first of) with the effect, light and spawn markers and the
  floor boxes' edges where they are drawn; in the Lights, Effects, Buildings, Landing zones and Spawns views that view's
  things. Each view's picker got a list of every candidate beside it, nearest the camera first (lights_wiring.js
  `lightsAt`, effects_view.js `markersNear` and effects_wiring.js `effectsAt`, spawns_wiring.js `spawnMarkersAt`,
  buildings_wiring.js `boxEdgesAt` and `volumesAt`, zones_wiring.js `zonesAt`); a click still takes the one it took. A
  spawn point or zone left picked in a view switched off doesn't count as selected in another. Gizmo handles are never
  in the list, so a piece behind them is reached. The diagonal cover marks are left out (one to a tile).
- **A piece of a group on its own** ([gizmo.js](../../tools/viewer/web/gizmo.js)): the gizmo keeps the picks taken out
  of their groups (`select`'s `alone`, `setSelection`'s `apart`, `picks().loose`, `looseKeys`). Such a pick takes its
  actor's parts, its assembly and its linked pieces (with that switch on), not its locked group: the gizmo moves and
  turns it alone, and Delete, Ctrl+C, the flags and the made pieces' panel act on it alone (a room's wall picked alone
  shows the Wall section). It stays a member of its group, and the mark stays through moves, undo and redo (the
  commands that put a selection back pass it: delete_reset.js, clipboard.js, buildings_wiring.js, builder_session.js)
  and goes with the next ordinary pick. Its box is the plain colour, and the group section says "One of the 12 pieces
  of this group, picked on its own · a click picks the group" ([groups.js](../../tools/viewer/web/groups.js)).
- The help panel ([index.html](../../tools/viewer/web/index.html)) and the
  [guide](../../tools/release/guide/guide.html) (a new section, "The piece behind: Ctrl and the wheel") say it;
  [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) has the area's row and its place in app.js's order.
