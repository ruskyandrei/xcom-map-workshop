### The guide says Clear map takes parts

The user guide's Clear map section ([guide.html](../../tools/release/guide/guide.html) `#clearmap`) describes the window
by parts: a tick for each part with what it takes and what stays (the pieces and ground, the lights, the effects and
sounds, the spawn points, the landing zones, the triggers and scripts), a part already cleared, the notes, the scenery
outside the play area kept per part, and what follows the pieces alone (a smaller grid, the Build's own floor
storeys, Long War's patch levels, the question what the map is for). The task "Make a new map on an old map's light
and landing" and the Map menu's list say it too. Its picture is taken again
([take_shots.py](../../tools/release/guide/take_shots.py) `clear_map` waits for the parts' counts): the window on the
Bar with every part ticked.
